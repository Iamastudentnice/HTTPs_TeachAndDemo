Glossary · A–Z

Key Words, Explained Simply

One line for each word used in the labs.

ABCDEHIKMPRST

A

ARP
How devices on a home network find each other's MAC address from an IP address. Lab 06
ARP spoofing
An attacker sends fake ARP replies so traffic flows through their device. Lab 06

B

Browser
The app you use to visit websites, such as Chrome, Safari or Firefox. Lab 01

C

Cache
A saved copy of something, kept so it does not have to be fetched again. Lab 02
Certificate
A website's ID card. It proves the website really owns its name. Lab 05
Certificate authority (CA)
A trusted organisation that checks and signs certificates. Lab 05
Certificate chain
The line of signatures from a website's certificate up to a trusted root CA. Lab 05
Ciphertext
Scrambled text produced by encryption. It looks like random characters. Lab 04

D

Decryption
Turning ciphertext back into readable text, using the right key. Lab 04
Digital signature
Proof, made with a private key, that a message is genuine and unchanged. Lab 04
DNS
The internet's phone book: it turns a name like example.com into an IP address. Lab 02

E

Eavesdropper
Someone who quietly watches network traffic without changing it. Lab 01
Encryption
Scrambling information so only someone with the right key can read it. Lab 04

H

Handshake
The first few messages that set up a connection before any data is sent. Lab 03
Hash
A short fingerprint of some data. Any change gives a completely different hash. Lab 04
HSTS
A website's instruction to browsers: always use HTTPS with me, never HTTP. Lab 06
HTTP
The language browsers and websites use to talk. It is not encrypted. Lab 01
HTTPS
HTTP sent through TLS, so it is encrypted while it travels. Lab 01

I

IP address
A number that identifies a device on a network, like a postal address. Lab 02

K

Key
The secret value used to encrypt or decrypt information. Lab 04

M

MAC address
The fixed hardware address of a network device on the local network. Lab 06
Man-in-the-middle (MITM)
An attacker who secretly sits between you and a website and relays the traffic. Lab 06
Metadata
Information about a message, such as who, where, when and how big - not its content. Lab 07

P

Packet
A small piece of data travelling across a network. Messages are sent in packets. Lab 01
Packet capture
A recording of the packets that passed through a network. Lab 07
Padlock
The browser's sign that the connection is protected. It does not mean the site is honest. Lab 01
Plaintext
Normal, readable text before it is encrypted. Lab 04
Port
A number that says which service a packet is for, e.g. 443 for HTTPS. Lab 07
Private key
The half of a key pair that is kept secret. It decrypts and signs. Lab 04
Public key
The half of a key pair that anyone may have. It encrypts and checks signatures. Lab 04

R

Root CA
A certificate authority your device trusts already. Every chain must end at one. Lab 05
Router
The box that connects your home network to the internet and passes data along. Lab 02

S

Server
The computer that runs a website and answers your browser. Lab 01
Session key
A fresh secret key, agreed during the handshake, used for one connection only. Lab 03

T

TCP
Makes sure every packet arrives, in order, so nothing is lost on the way. Lab 02
TLS
The protection behind HTTPS: it encrypts data and checks the website's certificate. Lab 03
TLS stripping
An attack that quietly downgrades a visit from HTTPS to plain HTTP. Lab 06
Trust store
The list of root CAs your device or browser trusts. Lab 05
