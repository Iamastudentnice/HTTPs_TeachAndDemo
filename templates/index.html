The three jobs of TLS

  • 1
    ConfidentialityData is encrypted, so eavesdroppers see only ciphertext.
  • 2
    IntegrityAuthenticated encryption detects any change made in transit.
  • 3
    AuthenticationThe certificate proves the server controls the domain name.
What HTTPS does not do
It does not prove a website is honest (scam sites have padlocks too), it cannot protect a device that already has malware, and it does not hide which servers you connect to.
