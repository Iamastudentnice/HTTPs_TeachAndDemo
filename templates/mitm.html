    IP vs MAC addresses, ARP requests, replies and tables
    
    
      
    IP address (192.168.1.1): a logical address used to route across networks. MAC address (00:11:22:33:44:01): the hardware address that actually delivers frames on the local network.
    
      
    ARP request: a broadcast asking "Who has 192.168.1.1?". ARP reply: "192.168.1.1 is at 00:11:…". Devices cache answers in their ARP table.
    
      
    ARP has no authentication, so a forged reply simply overwrites the table. ARP spoofing only works on the local network - and even then, HTTPS content stays encrypted.
