    Technical details: certificate authorities, chains and trust stores

    Certificate Authority (CA): an organisation that checks you control a domain before signing a certificate for it.

    Chain: site certificate ← signed by intermediate CA ← signed by root CA. The browser verifies every signature with the issuer's public key.

    Trust store: the small list of root CAs shipped with your browser/OS. If the chain doesn't end there, the site is untrusted.

    Remember: a valid certificate only proves you reached the real owner of the domain. Phishing sites can get valid certificates for their own look-alike domains.
